Live countdown and auditorium stage timer built for **DOOMFORGE** by **180 Degrees Consulting, VIT Chennai**. Styled after the official print poster with archival paper grain, Latverian emerald drafting accents, transparent partner logos, an animated orbital fracture canvas, and a mechanical 3D split-flap flip clock.

## Highlights

- **Transparent Partner Logo Bar:** Background-removed PNG logos for VIT Chennai, Unstop, ElevenLabs, OSEN, Adaption, and 180 Degrees Consulting VITC with automatic light/dark theme adaptation.
- **3D Mechanical Flip Clock:** Per-digit split-flap animation for `DAYS : HOURS : MINUTES : SECONDS` (defaults to **8 December 2026, 09:00 IST**).
- **Dual Mode Engine:** Switch between the live **Doomsday Countdown** and an auditorium **Stage Timer** with presets, `+1m`/`+5m` extensions, and inline editable phase labels.
- **Poster Paper & Auditorium Obsidian Themes:** Press `T` to toggle between the light editorial poster look and high-contrast dark mode for projector screens.
- **Orbital Fracture Canvas:** Rotating stippled planet, tectonic fracture vectors, concentric orbital rings, and parallax crosshairs that accelerate as time runs low.
